Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026

In short: Grand Game Collection does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app does not send any information over the internet.

What the app stores

To remember your games, the app saves a few things on your own device only: games in progress, statistics (games played, wins and best scores), favourites, recently played games and your settings, such as sound and card style. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, this information may be included in that backup, which Apple or your computer handles under your own settings.

What the app does not do

  • No sign-in or account of any kind.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools from us or any third party.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • No in-app purchases.

Deleting your data

You can clear all statistics in the app under Settings → Reset All. Deleting the app removes everything it has stored on that device.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Links

The app's settings link to this website. When you open a link, your browser visits grandgamecollection.com, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not use cookies or analytics on this website.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
