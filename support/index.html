Support

Found a bug, have a question or an idea for a game? Write to support@grandgamecollection.com. Please mention your device (iPhone, iPad or Mac), its system version and which game it is about. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I learn the rules of a game?

Open the game and tap the question mark in the toolbar. Every game has its own rules page.

How do I start a new game?

Use the new-game button in the game's toolbar. On iPhone, some actions sit behind the ••• button.

Can I turn off the sound?

Yes. Go to Settings and switch off Sound effects.

How do I reset my statistics?

Go to Settings → Reset All. This clears games played, wins and best scores for every game and cannot be undone.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Yes. Progress and statistics are stored only on your device, so deleting the app removes them. Restoring your device from a backup brings them back.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Every game works fully offline.

Does the app collect my data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
